The UMI Working Group

Mathematical methods for space sciences.

Space sciences — from celestial mechanics to cosmology, from planetary science to astrophysics — require a broad range of mathematical expertise. SPACEMATH was created to bring these competences together and to foster systematic interaction among members of the Italian Mathematical Union.

Why SPACEMATH

Mathematical analysis contributes to the solution of gravitational problems and trajectory optimization; the theory of differential equations and dynamical systems lies at the foundation of celestial mechanics; fluid dynamics and magnetohydrodynamics describe plasmas, planetary atmospheres and accretion disks. Statistical and probabilistic methods are essential for data interpretation, while numerical analysis supports computational models and simulations.

Mission

SPACEMATH aims to enable UMI members to interact in order to integrate and consolidate expertise in areas relevant to the solution of problems of broad interest to space sciences: from the study of natural bodies in the Solar System to the dynamics of artificial satellites, from cosmological problems to the trajectories of interplanetary probes.

Interdisciplinary vision. The group seeks to foster cross-fertilization among different mathematical disciplines, contributing to the consolidation of a national research community focused on topics of major scientific and technological interest.

Objectives

Systematic exchange among researchers with complementary expertise is intended to promote innovative methodological approaches and to consolidate research areas of strategic relevance both to mathematics and to space sciences.
