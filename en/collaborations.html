SPACEMATH · UMI Working Group

Collaborations

Mathematical methods for space sciences.

Industrial and non-academic collaborations are regarded as a key component of the success of the Working Group.

Interaction with the space sector

SPACEMATH intends to integrate interaction with researchers working in the space industry and space agencies into its activities. Some of the group proposers already have well-established experience of collaboration with industry, both on research topics and in advanced education.

Examples of existing collaborations

  • Italian Space Agency;
  • NEXT Ingegneria dei Sistemi;
  • SERCO;
  • SpaceDys;
  • Thales Alenia Space.

SPACEIND

For each of the three years of the group's activities, an annual meeting is planned among SPACEMATH participants, researchers from the space industry and the Italian Space Agency.

Outreach and training

SPACEDAY will help communicate complex scientific concepts to a non-specialist audience, with particular attention to young people and to the presentation of educational and professional pathways in mathematics.
