SPACEMATH · UMI Working Group

Activities

Mathematical methods for space sciences.

SPACEMATH will organize activities designed to promote the exchange of information, the interaction of complementary expertise, and communication among researchers, industry and the public.

E1 · Bimonthly webinar

A webinar every two months, followed by a discussion among participants.

E2 · SPACEMATH Workshop

One workshop each year on topics of interest to the Working Group.

E3 · SPACEIND

An annual meeting bringing together SPACEMATH participants, researchers from the space industry and the Italian Space Agency.

E4 · SPACEDAY

An annual public conference devoted to the role of mathematics in space sciences, also designed for secondary-school teachers and students.

Early-career researchers

PhD students and postdoctoral researchers will be invited to take part in activities E1–E4, helping to train a new generation of researchers in mathematical methods for space sciences and broadening opportunities for professional integration in academia, research centres, space agencies and industry.
